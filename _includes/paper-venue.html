{%- comment -%} Venue for listings that show the year in its own column. A venue that ends with that year loses it: "ICLR 2026" gives "ICLR", "Neurocomputing, 2025" gives "Neurocomputing". Any other venue is printed as written. The "|" marks the end so that only a trailing comma is removed. {%- endcomment -%} {%- capture shown_year -%}{% include paper-year.html post=include.post %}{%- endcapture -%} {%- assign venue_end = include.post.venue | slice: -4, 4 -%} {%- if venue_end == shown_year -%} {%- assign venue_length = include.post.venue | size | minus: 4 -%} {{- include.post.venue | slice: 0, venue_length | strip | append: "|" | remove: ",|" | remove: "|" -}} {%- else -%} {{- include.post.venue -}} {%- endif -%}
