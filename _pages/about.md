  I am a PhD student in Computer Science at [The Ohio State University](https://www.osu.edu/), advised by
  Prof. [Zhihui Zhu](https://zhihuizhu.github.io/){: .link--person }.
  My research examines how large language models represent concepts and how these representations evolve during
  generation and training. I am particularly interested in the linear structure of these representations and its
  role in understanding and steering model behavior. I also study how different training methods, such as SFT and RL,
  shape this structure.
  I received my B.S. in Computer Science from [UESTC](https://en.uestc.edu.cn/), where I worked with
  Prof. [Zhao Kang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=T_yCaN4AAAAJ&hl=en){: .link--person } on
